## How Protein % Is Calculated

> **Protein % of Calories** = (Protein grams x 4) / Total Calories x 100
>
> A product with 25g protein and 110 calories: (25 x 4) / 110 = **90.9%**
>
> **Target: >40% of calories from protein.**

## KEY INSIGHTS

### 1. Vital Wheat Gluten is #1 by a wide margin
At $0.011/g protein, it's 3-4x cheaper than the next best whole-food option (tofu). The trade-off is 30-60 minutes of processing time per batch. But you can make 2-3 lbs of seitan in one session, freeze it in portions, and use it all week.

### 2. TVP is the most underrated protein source
At $0.020/g protein with ZERO fat and 65% protein by calories, TVP is absurdly efficient. It rehydrates in 5 minutes and takes on whatever flavor you season it with. Look for "soya chunks" at Indian grocery stores for even cheaper prices.

### 3. Protein powders are competitive with whole foods
Soy protein isolate ($0.020/g) and pea protein ($0.021/g) are comparable to TVP in cost but offer 80-98% protein by calories. Good for smoothies and baking.

### 4. Tofu is a solid middle ground
At $0.038-0.050/g protein, tofu is 2-4x more expensive than VWG per gram of protein, but it requires zero processing and is extremely versatile. The Trader Joe's High Protein Super Firm is the best value if you want ready-to-cook tofu.

### 5. Pre-made plant-based meats are 5-12x more expensive than DIY
Upton's seitan ($0.090/g) costs 8x more per gram of protein than buying vital wheat gluten ($0.011/g) and making seitan yourself. You're paying for convenience and flavor.

### 6. Most branded "plant-based burgers" fail the protein test
Beyond Burger (35%), Impossible Burger (32%), and Gardein tenders (26%) all fail the >40% protein threshold due to high fat and carb content. They're designed to mimic the taste of meat, not maximize protein.

### 7. Field Roast is the best commercial plant-based meat for protein
At 42% protein by calories and $0.060/g, Field Roast sausages are the only major plant-based meat brand that clears the 40% threshold while still being reasonably priced.

## PRICE NOTES & CAVEATS

- Prices marked with ~ are estimates based on web research as of June 2026. Many retailer sites (Amazon, Walmart, Safeway, Giant) dynamically price products and blocked automated price extraction.
- Instacart typically marks up in-store prices by 5-15%. In-store shopping at Giant Food (3 blocks away) or Trader Joe's will save on both markup and delivery fees.
- Amazon Subscribe & Save can reduce prices by 5-15% on items like Anthony's VWG, protein powders, and PBfit.
- Costco membership ($65/year) may be worth it if you buy tofu and edamame in bulk regularly.
- H Mart via Instacart delivers Asian tofu brands that are often $1.49-2.49 per package -- worth checking for the cheapest per-unit tofu.
- The DC Grocery Access Pilot (launched Feb 2026) offers free Instacart+ memberships to eligible DC residents -- check if you qualify for free delivery.
