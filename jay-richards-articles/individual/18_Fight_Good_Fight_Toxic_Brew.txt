Title: Fight the Good Fight Excerpt: The Toxic Witch's Brew of Wokeness
Authors: James Robison and Jay W. Richards
Date: February 2024
Source: The Stream
URL: https://stream.org/fight-the-good-fight-excerpt-the-toxic-witchs-brew-of-wokeness/

This is an excerpt from the book Fight the Good Fight by James Robison and Jay W. Richards. The passage addresses the convergence of ideological movements that the authors describe as "wokeness" -- a toxic combination of ideas that has captured institutions across American society.

Leaders in government, the media, the academy, and corporations have attacked the American project of ordered liberty, using causes like "equity," "sustainable development," and "diversity" as their weapons. These terms, which sound benign or even admirable in isolation, have been redefined and weaponized to advance a radical agenda that undermines the foundations of a free society.

"Equity" no longer means fairness or equal treatment under the law -- it means equal outcomes enforced by government power, regardless of individual choices, effort, or circumstances. "Sustainable development" has become a framework for centralized economic planning and the restriction of individual liberty in the name of environmental goals. "Diversity" no longer refers to genuine intellectual or cultural diversity but to a rigid system of identity categories in which people are valued or devalued based on their membership in favored or disfavored groups.

The authors argue that these ideological currents have merged into a potent brew that threatens the American experiment in self-governance. What makes this brew particularly dangerous is that it has been adopted not just by fringe activists but by mainstream institutions -- corporations, universities, media organizations, and government agencies. The result is a pervasive ideological conformity that punishes dissent and rewards compliance.

Robison and Richards call on Christians and other Americans who value liberty to resist this ideological capture -- not by retreating from public life but by engaging courageously and thoughtfully. Fighting the good fight requires understanding the nature of the threat, speaking truthfully about it, and working to restore the principles of ordered liberty that have made America a beacon of freedom.
