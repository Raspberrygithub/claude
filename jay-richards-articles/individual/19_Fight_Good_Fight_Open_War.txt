Title: Fight the Good Fight Excerpt: Open War
Authors: James Robison and Jay W. Richards
Date: February 2024
Source: The Stream
URL: https://stream.org/fight-the-good-fight-excerpt-open-war-whether-you-would-risk-it-or-not/

This is a book excerpt from Fight the Good Fight by James Robison and Jay W. Richards. The passage argues that Americans who cherish liberty and traditional values are engaged in an open war for the future of their civilization, whether they would risk it or not.

The authors contend that the culture war is no longer a metaphor -- it is a reality that affects every institution, every community, and every family. The forces arrayed against the American tradition of ordered liberty are not content to coexist with those who hold traditional views. They seek to transform every institution in accordance with their ideology, and they are willing to use coercion, social pressure, and institutional power to achieve their goals.

Robison and Richards argue that many Americans have been slow to recognize the nature and scale of this conflict. Some have hoped that by staying quiet, avoiding controversy, and minding their own business, they could escape the culture war. But this is no longer possible. The war has come to them -- in their children's schools, in their workplaces, in their churches, and in their government.

The excerpt calls on readers to recognize the stakes, summon their courage, and engage in the fight. This does not necessarily mean political activism, though that may be part of it. It means being willing to speak the truth, to stand up for their convictions, and to accept the costs that may come with doing so. The authors draw on historical and biblical examples to argue that the willingness to fight for what is right -- even at personal cost -- is essential to preserving a free and just society.
