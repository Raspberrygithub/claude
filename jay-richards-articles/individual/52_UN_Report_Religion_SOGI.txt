Title: UN Report Castigates Religion for Stymieing the Sexual Orientation-Gender Identity Agenda
Author: Jay W. Richards and Grace Melton
Date: July 10, 2023
Source: The Daily Signal / Heritage Foundation
URL: https://www.dailysignal.com/2023/07/10/uns-sexual-orientation-gender-identity-czar-attacks-religion-hindrance-lgbtq-agenda/

The UN's independent "expert" on sexual orientation and gender identity capped off "Pride Month" with a report to the Human Rights Council addressing conflicts between religious freedom and LGBTQ+ rights — with religious freedom losing.

The report effectively treats religious conviction as an obstacle to be overcome rather than a fundamental human right to be protected. This is a dangerous inversion of the traditional understanding of human rights, in which religious freedom has always been considered among the most fundamental freedoms.

The UN SOGI expert's framing pits religious liberty against the newer, contested category of sexual orientation and gender identity rights, and resolves the conflict by subordinating the former to the latter. This approach is not neutral — it represents a clear ideological commitment to gender ideology that many of the world's religious traditions, including Christianity, Judaism, and Islam, reject.

The report castigates religious communities and leaders for maintaining traditional teachings on sex, marriage, and the human body. It treats these longstanding moral and theological positions as forms of discrimination rather than expressions of deeply held religious convictions that have shaped civilizations for millennia.

What makes this particularly concerning is the report's implicit suggestion that governments should use their power to override religious teachings and practices that conflict with the SOGI agenda. This would represent a fundamental assault on religious liberty — the "first freedom" that historically has been understood as a precondition for all other freedoms.
