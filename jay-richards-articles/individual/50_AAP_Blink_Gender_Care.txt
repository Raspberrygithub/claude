Title: Did American Academy of Pediatrics Just Blink on 'Gender Affirming Care' or Bluff?
Author: Jay W. Richards
Date: August 24, 2022
Source: Washington Stand / Daily Signal
URL: https://washingtonstand.com/commentary/did-american-academy-of-pediatrics-just-blink-on-gender-affirming-care-or-bluff

In its staring contest with human biology, did the American Academy of Pediatrics just blink?

For years, the AAP had been a booster for the "school-to-sterilization pipeline" for kids struggling with their sexed bodies. The AAP and other partisans "camouflage the harsh bits" of the treatment pipeline, referring to everything from social transition to sterilizing surgery as "gender affirming care." Its 2018 policy statement was "in effect, a catechism for this approach."

A telling letter to the Wall Street Journal seemed to signal a possible shift in the group's position.

Countries such as the United Kingdom, Sweden, and Finland have begun to have second thoughts about the "gender-affirming" protocol. Some evidence suggests children who initially identify as transgender will grow out of it as they mature. For many kids, puberty itself seems to be the cure for childhood gender dysphoria. The long-term effects of puberty blockers and cross-sex hormones are unknown, and the synthetic blockers are used off-label.

The AAP had been accused of stonewalling debate on hormone drugs and puberty blockers as some member doctors pushed back against a drug-first approach for children with gender dysphoria, through a document titled "In Support of a Rigorous Systematic Review of Evidence and Policy Update for Management of Pediatric Gender Dysphoria" (Resolution 27).

Parents and the general public should be outraged by this stonewalling. Medical organizations exist to serve patients, not ideological agendas. When the evidence points in one direction and the policy points in another, it is the policy that must change — not the evidence.
