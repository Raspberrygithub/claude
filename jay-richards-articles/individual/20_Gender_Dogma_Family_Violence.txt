Title: Democrats are Trying to Sneak Gender Dogma Into Bill on Family Violence
Author: Jay W. Richards
Date: October 2021
Source: The Stream
URL: https://stream.org/democrats-are-trying-to-sneak-gender-dogma-into-bill-on-family-violence/

Richards argues that Democrats are using legislation ostensibly aimed at addressing family violence as a vehicle for embedding gender ideology into federal law. The bill in question, while presented as a straightforward measure to combat domestic violence, contains provisions that would advance gender ideology under the cover of protecting vulnerable people.

The strategy is a familiar one: attach ideological provisions to legislation with a sympathetic purpose, so that anyone who opposes the ideological content can be accused of opposing the sympathetic purpose. In this case, opposing the insertion of gender dogma into family violence legislation is reframed as opposing protections for victims of family violence.

Richards examines the specific provisions of the bill that advance gender ideology, including language that redefines sex-based categories to include gender identity, provisions that would direct federal funds to organizations that promote gender transition, and requirements that shelters and services for victims of domestic violence accommodate individuals based on their claimed gender identity rather than their biological sex.

The article argues that these provisions are not merely symbolic -- they have real consequences for the women and children the legislation claims to protect. Women's shelters that serve traumatized women fleeing violent men would be required to admit biological males who claim a female gender identity. This undermines the very purpose of sex-segregated shelters and puts vulnerable women at additional risk.

Richards calls on lawmakers to separate the legitimate goal of combating family violence from the ideological agenda being smuggled into the legislation. Protecting victims of domestic violence should not be contingent on accepting gender ideology.
