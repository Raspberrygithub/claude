Title: Democrats Erase Women Through Budget 'Reconciliation'
Author: Jay W. Richards
Date: October 2021
Source: The Heritage Foundation
URL: https://www.heritage.org/gender/commentary/democrats-erase-women-through-budget-reconciliation

Richards examines how the $3.5 trillion tax-and-spend bill offered an opportunity for gender activists to incorporate ideological language into federal legislation, effectively erasing women from the law even in contexts that are exclusively about women's health.

The "Maternal Mortality" section (Part 4 of Subtitle J of Title III) consists of 15 sections appropriating funds related to women's health, yet uses gender-neutral terminology throughout. The phrase "pregnant, lactating, and postpartum individuals" is repeated 18 times in a section ostensibly about maternal health -- health that, by definition, pertains exclusively to women.

Richards pointedly asks: "Aren't Democrats supposed to be the party of feminists?" The irony is stark. A political party that has long claimed to champion women's rights is now systematically removing the word "woman" from legislation dealing with uniquely female experiences like pregnancy, lactation, and postpartum recovery.

This linguistic shift is not merely cosmetic, Richards argues. Language shapes how we think about reality, and when the law ceases to recognize women as a distinct category, the legal protections that depend on that category are weakened. If anyone can be a "pregnant individual," then the unique experiences and vulnerabilities of women are obscured.

The move reflects the growing influence of gender ideology within the Democratic Party. Under this ideology, the categories of "man" and "woman" are not rooted in biology but in self-identification. To maintain consistency with this view, legislators must strip gendered language from the law -- even when doing so produces absurdities like a "Maternal Mortality" section that never mentions mothers or women.

Richards argues that this represents a fundamental conflict between feminism and gender ideology. The two cannot coexist, because gender ideology dissolves the very category -- women -- that feminism exists to protect and advance.
