Title: Gender Ideology as State Education Policy
Author: Jay W. Richards (co-authored)
Date: December 2024
Source: The Heritage Foundation
URL: https://www.heritage.org/education/report/gender-ideology-state-education-policy

This co-authored Heritage Foundation report provides a comprehensive analysis of state-level education frameworks across all 50 states regarding transgender-related lessons and policies for children. It represents one of the most thorough surveys of how gender ideology has been incorporated into public education at the state level.

The report examines state education standards, guidance documents, model curricula, and policy directives to assess the extent to which gender ideology has been embedded in K-12 education across the country. It catalogs which states have adopted policies requiring or encouraging instruction on gender identity, which states have adopted policies protecting children from such instruction, and which states have no clear policy in either direction.

The findings reveal a patchwork of approaches. Some states have aggressively incorporated gender ideology into their education frameworks, mandating that students be taught concepts such as the gender spectrum, gender fluidity, and the distinction between sex and gender identity from early grades. Other states have enacted protections for parents and children, restricting instruction on sexual orientation and gender identity in elementary schools or requiring parental notification and consent.

The report argues that gender ideology in education is not a neutral or scientific approach to human sexuality but an ideological framework that contradicts biological reality and can cause significant harm to children. Teaching children that they may have been born in the wrong body, that their sex was merely "assigned" at birth, or that medical interventions can change their sex is not education -- it is indoctrination.

The authors provide recommendations for state policymakers seeking to protect children from gender ideology in schools while ensuring that education remains grounded in biological reality and respectful of parental rights.
