Title: The Battle Over Parents' Rights in Education Is Just Getting Started
Author: Jay W. Richards
Date: November 2022
Source: The Heritage Foundation
URL: https://www.heritage.org/education/commentary/the-battle-over-parents-rights-education-just-getting-started

Parents across the country are discovering that schools are teaching their children that an internal sense of gender trumps biological sex. This discovery has ignited a growing movement of parents demanding transparency and accountability from the institutions entrusted with their children's education.

Critical race theory may have been the issue that first drew parents' attention to what was happening in classrooms, but it is far less shocking than the radical gender ideology that seems to have overtaken our nation's schools. While CRT teaches children to see everything through the lens of race, gender ideology goes further -- it teaches children that their own bodies may be wrong, that they might have been "born in the wrong body," and that medical interventions can fix this perceived mismatch. For young children still developing their sense of self, these messages can be profoundly destabilizing.

The parental rights movement is not merely a reaction to one or two controversial lessons. It reflects a deeper recognition that many public schools have become vehicles for ideological indoctrination rather than genuine education. Schools have adopted policies that allow children to socially transition -- changing names, pronouns, and even using opposite-sex facilities -- without parental knowledge or consent. In some cases, school personnel have been explicitly instructed to hide this information from parents.

Universal school choice would not just make schools better and more competitive -- it would make them less of an arena for the culture war. When parents have the ability to choose where their children are educated, schools must be responsive to families rather than to ideological activists. Competition forces schools to focus on what parents actually want: a solid education in reading, writing, math, and science, delivered in an environment that respects their values and their role as primary educators of their children.

The battle over parents' rights in education is just getting started. As more parents become aware of what is being taught in their children's schools, the demand for transparency, accountability, and choice will only grow.
