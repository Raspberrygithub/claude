Title: Stand Together For Freedom
Author: Jay W. Richards
Date: Undated
Source: The Stream
URL: https://stream.org/stand-together-for-freedom/

Richards issues a call for Americans who value freedom to unite across their differences and stand together against the growing threats to liberty in American life. The article argues that the forces arrayed against individual freedom, religious liberty, and constitutional government are too powerful to be resisted by any single group acting alone.

The article addresses the tendency of freedom-loving Americans to fracture along ideological, religious, and cultural lines -- allowing their disagreements with one another to prevent effective cooperation against common threats. Richards argues that while genuine differences exist and should not be papered over, the stakes are too high for the luxury of internal conflict.

Religious believers, classical liberals, libertarians, and traditional conservatives all have reason to be alarmed by the growth of government power, the erosion of free speech, the ideological capture of institutions, and the assault on the family. These groups may disagree about many things, but they share a common interest in preserving the conditions for a free society -- conditions that are now under serious threat.

Richards calls for a renewed commitment to the principles that have made America a beacon of freedom: limited government, the rule of law, the protection of individual rights, and respect for the mediating institutions of civil society. These principles are not the exclusive property of any one political faction -- they belong to the American tradition as a whole, and their defense requires the broadest possible coalition.

The article urges readers to set aside secondary disagreements, focus on the most urgent threats, and work together to preserve the freedoms that previous generations fought to establish and protect.
