Title: Biden Doubles Down on Radical 'Gender-Affirming Care' for Kids
Author: Jay W. Richards
Date: April 2022
Source: The Daily Signal / Heritage Foundation
URL: https://www.dailysignal.com/2022/04/01/biden-doubles-down-on-radical-gender-affirming-care-for-kids/

Biden's Justice Department sent a letter to all state attorneys general warning that they could be violating civil rights laws if they keep minors from receiving "gender-affirming care." The HHS guide was described as "vague and bureaucratic," but it spelled out interventions including social affirmation "at any age," puberty blockers "during puberty," and more invasive procedures at later stages.

The term "gender-affirming care" is a euphemism for giving hormones to children and, in many cases, performing irreversible surgery. The Biden White House was "following radical left activists" on this issue.

States like Texas had begun pushing back — the Texas attorney general ruled that procedures like giving a girl heavy doses of testosterone or a double mastectomy for gender-affirming purposes could constitute child abuse. This represented an important first step in state-level resistance to the federal government's attempt to normalize experimental and irreversible medical interventions on minors.

The Biden administration's approach represented a significant escalation in the push to make gender ideology the default framework for pediatric medicine. Rather than following the precautionary principle — which would counsel caution when the long-term effects of these interventions remain largely unknown — the administration was actively encouraging and threatening legal consequences for states that tried to protect children from potentially harmful treatments.

This is not how a responsible government handles an area of active scientific uncertainty. Countries like the United Kingdom, Sweden, and Finland were already pulling back from the aggressive "affirm-only" model. The Biden administration was moving in the opposite direction, making the United States an outlier among its peer nations.
