Title: A Court Victory in Texas Against Obamacare's Transgender Mandate
Author: Jay W. Richards and Sarah Parshall Perry
Date: August 19, 2021
Source: The Daily Signal
URL: https://www.dailysignal.com/2021/08/19/a-court-victory-in-texas-against-obamacares-transgender-mandate

A judge's ruling in Texas means that medical professionals may continue to follow their moral, religious, and professional convictions in treating patients when gender issues are involved.

This court victory represents an important check on the federal government's attempt to weaponize anti-discrimination law to force medical professionals to participate in gender transition procedures against their conscience and clinical judgment.

The Affordable Care Act's Section 1557 prohibits sex discrimination in healthcare. The Obama administration interpreted "sex" to include "gender identity," effectively creating a transgender mandate that would require doctors, hospitals, and insurers to provide and cover gender transition procedures — including puberty blockers, cross-sex hormones, and surgeries — regardless of the medical professional's clinical assessment or religious convictions.

The Texas court recognized what should be obvious: compelling a doctor to perform a procedure that conflicts with his or her best medical judgment is not the proper function of anti-discrimination law. Medical professionals should be free to treat patients according to the best available evidence and their professional training, not according to an ideological framework imposed by federal bureaucrats.

This ruling is a victory for religious liberty, medical conscience, and common sense. It should serve as a model for other courts grappling with similar questions about the scope of anti-discrimination protections and the limits of government power to override professional medical judgment.
