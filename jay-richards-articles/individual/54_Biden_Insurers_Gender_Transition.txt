Title: Biden Proposal Would Force Insurers to Pay for Gender 'Transition'
Author: Jay W. Richards and Jared Eckert
Date: February 4, 2022
Source: The Daily Signal
URL: https://www.dailysignal.com/2022/02/04/bidens-latest-proposal-would-force-insurers-to-pay-for-gender-transition/

President Joe Biden sought to inject gender ideology into laws since his first day in office, preferring a bill like the Equality Act. With that effort stalled in the Senate, his administration looked for alternative ways to insert sexual orientation and gender identity concepts into existing law.

The latest proposal would force insurers to cover gender transition procedures, effectively requiring all insurance policyholders to subsidize experimental medical interventions that many consider harmful and that conflict with the best available medical evidence.

This represents yet another attempt by the Biden administration to advance gender ideology through administrative action rather than through the legislative process. Unable to pass the Equality Act through Congress, the administration has turned to executive orders, agency regulations, and creative reinterpretation of existing laws to achieve the same goals.

The proposal illustrates a broader pattern: the use of federal regulatory power to normalize and subsidize gender transition procedures while simultaneously making it more difficult for states, medical professionals, and insurers to exercise caution or apply their own clinical and moral judgment.

Insurance mandates of this kind raise fundamental questions about the proper scope of government authority, the rights of conscience, and the relationship between ideology and medicine. When the government compels private insurers to cover specific procedures — particularly controversial ones with uncertain long-term outcomes — it substitutes political judgment for clinical judgment and removes important safeguards that exist for the protection of patients.
