Title: The White House Plan to Make Gender Ideology a Central Theme of the American Experiment
Author: Jay W. Richards
Date: Heritage Backgrounder (undated)
Source: The Heritage Foundation
URL: https://www.heritage.org/gender/report/the-white-house-plan-make-gender-ideology-central-theme-the-american-experiment

This Heritage Foundation Backgrounder analyzes the Biden White House's National Strategy on Gender Equity and Equality, a sweeping policy document that seeks to embed gender ideology into virtually every aspect of federal governance.

The elevated rhetoric of "equity" and "equality" cloaks a darker agenda and sets the government on a revolutionary path that breaks with the American tradition of legal rights and equality. The American founding was built on the principle that all individuals possess inherent rights and deserve equal treatment under the law. The Biden administration's gender strategy replaces this principle with a framework rooted in identity politics and group-based outcomes.

The National Strategy on Gender Equity and Equality goes far beyond traditional efforts to ensure equal opportunity for women. It redefines "gender" to include "gender identity," thereby collapsing the distinction between biological sex and subjective self-perception. This redefinition has far-reaching consequences for law, policy, education, health care, and the military.

Under this framework, policies designed to protect women based on their biological sex are reinterpreted to include anyone who identifies as a woman, regardless of biology. This effectively nullifies sex-based protections in areas such as athletics, prisons, shelters, and health care. It also mandates that federal agencies, contractors, and grant recipients adopt gender identity policies as a condition of doing business with the government.

Richards argues that the strategy represents a fundamental transformation of the relationship between the individual and the state. Rather than protecting individual rights and freedoms, the government positions itself as the arbiter of identity and the enforcer of ideological conformity. Institutions that resist -- whether religious organizations, schools, or health care providers -- face the threat of losing federal funding, contracts, or legal protections.

The Backgrounder details the specific policy proposals embedded in the strategy and their likely consequences for American institutions, families, and individual liberty. It serves as both an analysis of the strategy's content and a warning about the direction in which it would take the country.
