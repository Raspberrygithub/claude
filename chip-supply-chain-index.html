The Chip Supply Chain, Visualized

Three interactive maps of the global semiconductor industry. Circle size encodes market cap; layers run from sand to silicon to AI. Built with D3 — open in any modern browser, scroll to zoom, drag to pan.
Map 1 · Industry-wide

◎ Global Semiconductor Supply Chain

Every layer of the industry, from Shin-Etsu wafers to Apple's iPhone: materials → equipment → EDA → fabless → IDMs → memory → foundries → OSAT → distribution → end-buyers. ~120 companies.

10 layers ~120 companies $15T+ tracked mcap
Map 2 · AI-specific

◤ AI Chip Supply Chain

Zoomed in on what builds GPT and Gemini: AI accelerator designers, HBM memory, CoWoS advanced packaging, AI networking, server ODMs, hyperscalers and frontier labs. Critical chokepoints flagged in red.

Map 3 · Geopolitics

◆ Geopolitical Concentration

Where each layer of the chain physically lives. Country shares per layer — making the brittle dependencies visible: 100% of EUV in the Netherlands, ~90% of leading-edge logic in Taiwan, ~90% of HBM in Korea.

18 layers 10 countries 9 chokepoint layers

What every map gives you

  • Pan + zoom across a very large canvas — fit-to-screen button to reset
  • Hover any node for company description, country, market cap, role in the chain
  • Click a node to highlight only its supply-chain connections
  • Search by company name; filter by country / region
  • Circle size = sqrt-scaled market cap so $5B and $3T are both visible
Market caps are approximate early-2025 figures, rounded to highlight scale rather than precision. Country-share percentages come from public industry reports (TrendForce, IC Insights, SEMI, company filings) and represent best-effort estimates of global capacity or revenue share. For diversified parents (Samsung, Sony, Siemens, Hon Hai) the conglomerate market cap is shown.
